These 15 SVGs identify the respective universities. School names, emblems, artwork and trademark rights remain with their respective institutions. Inclusion does not imply affiliation, endorsement, or a school-issued authorization. The skill's software MIT license does not relicense these marks, and this directory does not represent them as unrestricted MIT/CC0 artwork.

No school emblem was redrawn. All output files remain vectors.

## Intended identification and official rules

Use school marks beside accurate written names to identify real educational background. Preserve proportions and official visual identity requirements; avoid using a school's mark as this software's own logo or implying approval of a resume or product. Check the relevant institution's rules for the intended public or commercial context.

For BUPT, [the official VIS](https://vi.bupt.edu.cn/) and [校徽规范](https://vi.bupt.edu.cn/jcxt/xhgf.htm) describe standard artwork, colors, clear space, backgrounds and prohibited forms. The bundled SVG is a third-party vector rendition from the pinned collection, not represented as an official university-issued download. The checked official pages retain BUPT copyright; this record does not claim an unrestricted redistribution grant. No express blanket prohibition on this collection's redistribution was found in the checked upstream license/README. This is a limited source review, not a claim that all institution-specific uses have been cleared.

Therefore **none of those earlier downloaded bytes is used in this public directory**. The public school assets were independently obtained from the pinned soulteary collection above. Existing user-local downloads are not deleted, and their own recorded rights notices still apply.
